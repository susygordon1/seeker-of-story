Who I Was · The Before

25 Years of Corporate.
None of it was wasted.

My career started in high school at Mesquite ISD in television broadcasting — putting programming on the air. That was the first chapter. I just didn't know it yet.

What followed was 25 years in corporate America. Business Analyst. Project Manager. Scrum Master. Agile Transformation Coach. Director of Strategy and Delivery in Tech Consulting. I climbed. I delivered. I led teams. I managed transformations for organizations that didn't always know they needed one.

I was earning $155,000 a year. I had the titles. I had the resume. And I had a growing sense that none of it was what I was actually built for.

None of it was wasted. Every skill I built in those 25 years is in this platform right now — the project management, the agile framework, the strategy, the media production, the storytelling. God was building the toolbox the whole time. I just didn't know what He was building it for.

What I'm Building

Two platforms.
One mission.

Mega Mission Media is the production engine — a faith-based digital media company helping businesses build their presence through websites, social media, content production, video, and AI solutions. This is how the mission is funded.

Seeker of Story is the impact platform — a free, searchable archive of founder blueprints and real transformation stories. No paywalls. No subscriptions. No gatekeeping. Built for the person who needs a mentor at 2 AM and can't afford a mastermind.

One earns. One gives. Together they are the ecosystem.

Why I'm Building It

I made a promise.
This is me keeping it.

On October 31, 2023, addiction left my body instantly. No shakes. No shivers. No desires. Gone. In that moment I made a promise to God — I will tell everybody what You did in my life.

For three years I drove rideshare through Fort Worth. I gave out Baggies of Hope. I gave out Bibles and devotionals. I gave out prayers and hugs and the Word written on index cards. I was a one-on-one blueprint machine in the front seat of a car.

When I became a founder, I needed mentorship and couldn't find a real, free, ungated place to get it. LinkedIn is accolades. Masterminds cost thousands. I needed someone who had already walked my exact path — and there was no database for that.

So I built one.

How I'm Building It

The Blueprint.
No filters. No gatekeeping.

This is the part that speaks life. Not what I built — how I built it. Take what applies. Leave what doesn't.

01
Fasting & Prayer — First and Always
Before every major decision, every pitch, every build — I fast and I pray. Not as a ritual. As a reset. It clears the noise and sharpens the direction. Everything else on this list flows from this one.
02
Pick Up the Phone — Even When You Don't Know What You're Selling
After my car wreck, I made 30 calls in one day. I didn't have a pitch deck. I didn't have a website. I just started connecting. Two callbacks. Two appointments. One closed deal. The attorney client who became my first case study. You don't need to know everything before you start. You just need to start.
03
Network — Especially Before You're Ready
Get on the One Million Cups circuit. Present your idea before it's fully formed — the feedback IS the build. I've done the full DFW circuit. Every room I walked into gave me something. Even when I didn't have all the answers, showing up consistently built the credibility that comes before the clients.
04
Fund It With What You Have
I drove rideshare while building this. My car was my studio, my office, my prayer closet, and my income source simultaneously. Don't wait for the perfect funding situation. Use what's in your hands right now. The provision comes as you move.
05
Use Every Tool Available to You
I am not a developer. I am not a graphic designer. But I built this platform using every tool available — and I'm still learning every day.
HeyGen ChatGPT Claude Gemini Squarespace GitHub Vercel YouTube Studio Podcast Production Media & Broadcasting Agile / Scrum Project Management
06
Document Everything — Your Story Is the Product
Every episode. Every passenger. Every founder conversation. Every One Million Cups presentation. Every setback. I documented it. The YouTube channel IS the platform. The stories are not the marketing — they are the mission. Whatever you're building, document the becoming. Someone needs your story more than your finished product.
