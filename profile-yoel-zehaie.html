Who They Were · The Before

The path that
built the founder.

Yoel Zehaie completed law school with the knowledge and the credential. He did what was required — studied, passed the bar, earned the degree. Law school gives you the tools. What it does not give you is the roadmap for what comes next.

The path from graduate to firm founder is one that nobody fully prepares you for. The business side. The client side. The personal side. Yoel figured it out.

What They Built

The business.
Built from nothing.

Zehaie Law — serving Dallas, Tarrant, and surrounding counties in criminal, family, and civil law. Yoel is a dedicated advocate, especially for fathers fighting for their rights in custody battles and family disputes.

Phone: 682-336-5127 · Website: CallYoelNow.com

Why They Built It

The reason
that drives it.

Yoel doesn't just handle cases. He stands with people through some of the most important moments of their lives. That level of care doesn't come from a textbook — it comes from understanding what it means to build something from scratch, to take a risk, and to be accountable to real people with real stakes.

He built his firm because he believed he could serve clients differently. Not just as a lawyer — as an advocate.
