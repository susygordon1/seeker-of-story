Homeless on her 47th Birthday
→ Faith & Restoration

On her 47th birthday, with no food, no shelter, and just $2 in her pocket, Brenda sat down and shared her story. After the loss of her mother, a broken marriage, and being left with nothing, Brenda found herself homeless on the streets of Fort Worth — yet her spirit was unbroken.

She survived trauma, assault, and abandonment by the system. And on her hardest day, she still gave her last dollar to someone else. That is who Brenda is.

Her story is not finished. It is being written right now — one day at a time, in faith, on the streets of Fort Worth, Texas.
