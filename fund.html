What This Is Becoming
FORT WORTH FITNESS SANCTUARY
A gym open to all.
Open to the Home Free.

God showed Susy a fully operational community gym — open to anyone, including the homeless — funded entirely by sponsorships. The front door is fitness. Personal trainers. Movement. The mind getting right. Because that's where transformation begins.

But the back rooms are where the mission lives. A mental health professional. A city housing advocate. A spiritual guide. A wraparound support team. Every person who walks through that door gets more than a workout — they get seen, heard, and resourced.

Think of a church — where sick people go to get better. This is that. Disguised as a gym. Funded by the community. Built by obedience. Sustained by story.

The digital platform you're on right now — the stories, the seekers, the founders — this is the foundation that builds that gym. Every story told. Every seat filled. Every donor who fuels this mission. It all points here.
