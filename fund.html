What This Is Becoming
Fort Worth Fitness Sanctuary
A gym open to all.
Open to the Home Free.

God showed Susy a fully operational community gym — open to anyone, including the homeless — funded entirely by sponsorships. The front door is fitness. Personal trainers. Movement. The mind getting right. Because that is where transformation begins.

But the back rooms are where the mission lives. A mental health professional. A city housing advocate. A spiritual guide. A wraparound support team. Every person who walks through that door gets more than a workout — they get seen, heard, and resourced.

Think of a church — where sick people go to get better. This is that. Funded by the community. Built by obedience. Sustained by story.

The digital platform you are on right now — the stories, the seekers, the founders — this is the foundation that builds that sanctuary. Every story told. Every seat filled. Every donor who fuels this mission. It all points here.

501(c)(3) Establishment
Speak Life Transformation Ministry has its EIN and formation documentation. The next step is applying for 501(c)(3) status — which unlocks tax-deductible donations, grants, and the full nonprofit infrastructure this mission is called to operate under. If you are a nonprofit attorney or have navigated this process, your guidance is needed.
Most Urgent

Legal and Business Structure
Mega Mission Media is currently an unincorporated DBA with a bank account. The goal is a clean, properly structured media entity — separate from the nonprofit arm — that can generate revenue, enter contracts, and grow the platform. If you are a business attorney or CPA who works with faith-based organizations, this is your seat.
