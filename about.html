Our Mission

Why Seeker of Story Exists

When you're in transition, unemployed, burned out, or standing at the edge of a leap — you can't afford high, overrated, astronomical monthly subscriptions. But somewhere out there is a founder who already walked your exact path — and is ready to share the blueprint.

"
I made a promise to God
that I would tell
everybody what He did.

On October 31, 2023 — a day I will never forget — addiction left my body instantly. No shakes. No shivers. No desires. Gone. In that moment I made a promise: I would tell everybody what God did in my life.

For three years I drove rideshare through Dallas and Fort Worth — giving out Baggies of Hope, Bibles, prayers, and blueprints one passenger at a time. I discovered something: everybody carries a story. Everybody carries a blueprint. And if we put them all in one place, we can elevate each other.

When I became a founder, I needed mentorship — and there was no free, real, ungated place to find it. So God said: I'm building something in you.

Seeker of Story is that something. A hope platform. A blueprint archive. Free for every seeker who needs it. Always.

— Susy Gordon, Founder · Mega Mission Media · Seeker of Story
Susy Gordon · Founder
The Problem We're Solving

The Blueprint Has Always Existed

There are people in this world who have already done what you are trying to do. They've made the mistakes. They've found the path. They've built the thing from nothing. And they are willing — even desperate — to share what they know.

And then there are seekers. People in transition, in burnout, in the wilderness of a career change or a life collapse — who are spending money they don't have on masterminds, subscriptions, and courses that teach theory instead of testimony.

Seeker of Story is the bridge. A free, permanent, searchable archive of real founder blueprints — built from the front seat of a car, one honest conversation at a time.

We are not providing motivation. We are providing the map.

The Library of Scrolls

A Modern-Day Archive of Testimony

Every profile on this platform is cataloged as a modern scroll — a living record of transformation. Not a resume. Not a LinkedIn profile. A becoming story. The raw, real, unfiltered account of what it took to get from lost to launched.

These scrolls are permanent. They are searchable. And they are free — because the person who needs them at their lowest moment should never have to pay to find hope.

The Rule of Alignment
Every story on this platform must anchor the modern-day breakthrough to a truth that transcends it. The journey and the testimony become one. The scroll is not just a story — it is evidence of something larger at work.
