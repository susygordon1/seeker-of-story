Our Mission

Why Seeker of Story Exists

When you're in transition, unemployed, burned out, or standing at the edge of a leap, you can't afford high, overrated, astronomical monthly subscriptions. But somewhere out there is a founder who already walked your exact path — and is ready to become your mentor.

The Origin

Born on the Trinity Trail

One morning in Fort Worth, TX, Susy Gordon was walking the Trinity Trail. Three words came. Spoken three times: "Seek and find." Matthew 7:7. She didn't ignore it.

Seeker of Story is the platform that came from that moment. A free mentor-matching database where anyone in transition can search for a founder who walked their exact path — and find them.

It is built on one conviction: that testimony is the most powerful currency on earth, and that the founder who shares their story has already spoken life into someone who needed it.

The Model

Built on The Goodwill Principle

No revenue sharing. No accolades. Just a founder unknowingly becoming a voice of hope to someone who needed it.

01
Founders Give
Founders give because they're finally in a position to give freely. They share who they were, what they're building, why they're building it, and the lessons that shaped the journey.
02
SOS Becomes the Bridge
It carries stories, blueprints, wisdom, and lived experience from people willing to share what they've learned so someone else doesn't have to walk alone.
03
The Seeker Finds Hope
Anyone in transition can search the Becoming Database freely. No monthly subscriptions. No gatekeeping. No barriers. Just a seeker and a story.

"There is life and death in the power of the tongue, and the founder who shares their story has already spoken life."

The Anti-AA Model

From Anonymity to Activation

Traditional recovery and mentorship models are built on anonymity. What happens here stays here. The seat has no name.

Seeker of Story is the opposite. It is a culture of radical testimony. The mandate is to share loudly — to defeat the noise of the world with the sound of what God did.

Every story on this platform is a scroll. A living, digital archive of what happens when a person says yes to the becoming.

The Library of Scrolls

A Modern-Day Archive of Testimony

Every profile on this platform is cataloged as a modern scroll — a living record of God's moving hand. Not a resume. Not a LinkedIn profile. A becoming story.

The Rule of Alignment
To speak on the platform, a leader cannot just tell a story. They must explicitly anchor their modern-day breakthrough to a matching biblical narrative — mapping their journey directly to scriptural truth. The story and the scripture become one.
